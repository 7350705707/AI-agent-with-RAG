**Assistant:** White‑hat (or ethical) hacking is the practice of intentionally probing a system to discover security weaknesses so that those flaws can be fixed and the system’s defenses strengthened. It is also known as white‑hat hacking.  

The reference material splits this field into two primary components:  

1. **Penetration testing** – performed by legitimate, independent information‑security firms whose goal is to help organizations improve their security posture.  
2. **Military/Intelligence engagement** – where ethical hackers work for a nation’s armed forces or intelligence agencies to protect critical assets. Both areas are described as rapidly growing and in high demand. [Linux Basics for Hackers 2025 (Credits‑@cehv13grp) (4).pdf]
