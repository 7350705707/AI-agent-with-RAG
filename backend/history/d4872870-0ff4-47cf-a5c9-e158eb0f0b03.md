*Launched May 7–10, 2025, as a punitive response to the April 22, 2025 Pahalgam attack (26 civilians killed). India accused Pakistan of support and authorized Operation Sindoor—a four‑day tri‑service campaign targeting nine terror camps across the Line of Control (LoC) and within Pakistan.*  

**Objectives:**  
- Dismantle terror infrastructure (punitive).  
- Send a deterrent signal against cross‑border terrorism.  
- Demonstrate India’s capability for coordinated Army, Air Force, and Navy operations.  

**Execution:**  
- Precision strikes destroyed camps and supply lines; Indian Army’s 15 Corps led ground artillery shelling while the Air Force conducted missile/air strikes. The Navy maintained readiness to deter escalation. Pakistan’s Border Action Team (BAT) retaliation was repelled.  

**Outcome & Significance:**  
- Ceasefire agreed after heavy losses; terror networks severely disrupted across LoC.  
- Highlighted India’s modern joint warfare doctrine—precision over escalation, targeting only terrorist infrastructure.  
- Reinforced India’s deterrence posture against cross‑border terrorism and showcased its ability to conduct multi‑domain operations (since Balakot in 2019).
