  • B Cybersex trafficking
  • C Drug trafficking on dark-net markets
  • D Malware attacks

  • A Using computers to commit robbery
  • B Collecting personal information for ruining people's lives
  • C Tending to cause fear among people and demonstrating power
  • D Both B and C
Q 3 Which of the following best describes cybersex trafficking? Easy
  • A Sending unsolicited emails to victims
  • B The transportation and coercion of victims for sexual exploitation using digital means
  • C Hacking social media accounts for personal gain
  • D Installing spyware on a victim's device
Q 4 What is a Denial-of-Service (DoS) attack? Easy
  • A Overwhelming a system with traffic to make it unavailable to legitimate users
  • B Sending phishing emails to steal passwords
  • C Installing ransomware to encrypt files
  • D Stealing user identity to open bank accounts

  • A Firewall
  • B VPN tunnel
  • C Computer virus
  • D SSL certificate
Q 6 Cyberextortion is best defined as when: Medium
  • A A website or system is subjected to repeated denial-of-service attacks by hackers demanding payment
  • B Victims are coerced into paying ransom for the release of stolen or encrypted data
  • C A computer virus is used to steal classified government information
  • D Both A and B
Q 7 Which of the following is a form of cybercrime that specifically targets computers as the victim (not just as a tool)? Medium
  • A Identity theft
  • B Denial-of-service attacks
  • C Financial fraud crimes
  • D Cybersex trafficking
Q 8 Dark-net markets are primarily associated with which of the following? Medium
  • A Legitimate e-commerce for privacy-conscious consumers
  • B Government-regulated digital auctions
  • C Illegal activities such as drug trafficking and stolen data sales
  • D Secure corporate intranet communication
Q 9 What does the phrase "While Osama bin Laden may have his finger on the trigger, his grandson may have his finger on the mouse" imply about modern threats? Hard
  • A Traditional terrorism is more dangerous than cyberterrorism
  • B Cyberattacks can be just as destructive as traditional physical attacks
  • C The next generation will not need to use physical weapons at all
  • D Cybersecurity measures are less important than physical security
Q 10 Which of the following does NOT belong to the category of computer fraud? Hard
  • A Extortion through blackmailing via digital means
  • B Theft of classified information using hacking tools
  • C Financial fraud crimes carried out through online banking
  • D Drug trafficking on dark-net markets
